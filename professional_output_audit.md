# 🛡️ Professional Output Audit: Human Reviewer Standard

**1. The "Bullsh\*t" Detector**

- [ ] Is the seniority level accurate? (Junior vs. Leader).
- [ ] Are all dates and years identical to the original PDF?
- [ ] Are there any "fake" metrics that weren't in the original?

**2. Narrative Soundness**

- [ ] Does it sound like a human wrote it? (No "customer-obsessed" or "leveraging").
- [ ] Does the **Summary** get to the point in 3 lines or less?
- [ ] Does the text explain _how_ a tool was used, or just name-drop it?

**3. Data Integrity**

- [ ] Are the 'EXPERIENCE' and 'PROJECTS' sections fully populated?
- [ ] Are the section headers identical to the original resume?
